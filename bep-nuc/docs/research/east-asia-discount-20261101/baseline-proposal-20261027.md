## 5 Revenue model and go to market plan


## How Bếp Núc earns revenue

Revenue comes from a negotiated price gap. Before listing, each shop agrees in writing a fixed discount d on the menu price P of its eligible surplus; we will target 25–30%. For every completed portion the shop is paid that settled amount, F = (1 − d) × P, whichever voucher the student chooses. The shop therefore knows its income per portion in advance.

On the platform the student sees a smaller default offer: a 5% base reduction on every eligible meal, plus one optional evening voucher v of 7%, 10% or 20%. Bếp Núc keeps the spread between what the student pays and what the shop receives. Vouchers are funded from this spread, not from the shop.

> Student pays T = ceil[0.95 × P × (1 − v)]   ·   Shop receives F = (1 − d) × P   ·   Bếp Núc keeps S = T − F before costs

For example, if a shop agrees a 30% discount and a student uses the 10% voucher, the student pays 85.5% of the menu price, the shop receives 70% and Bếp Núc keeps about 15%.

| Voucher chosen | Student pays (% of menu) | Spread with 25% agreement | Spread with 30% agreement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No voucher (5% base only) | 95.0% | 20.0% | 25.0% |
| 7% | 88.4% | 13.4% | 18.4% |
| 10% | 85.5% | 10.5% | 15.5% |
| 20% | 76.0% | 1.0% | 6.0% |

A 7% or 10% voucher leaves a usable spread under either agreement. A 20% voucher leaves only 1–6% of the menu price, too little to cover handling on a typical student meal. The discount negotiated with each shop therefore decides which vouchers an item can accept.

## Go to market and validation

We will recruit supply before promoting volume. Discovery targets about 70 students and interviews with 10–20 shops around candidate Hanoi campuses. These are planned samples rather than completed research. Ask students about recent purchases, actual spend, usual alternatives and pickup constraints. Document shops’ menu evidence, surplus by closing shift, current recovery channels, desired payout, storage and staff effort.

Compare candidate Hanoi campus areas and select the campus cluster with the most qualifying shops within 2 km; use student trial registrations to resolve ties. Observe at least a week of surplus, including shifts with none. Do not extrapolate the discovery sample into Vietnam market demand. Three shops offering five portions over 20 evenings would represent 300 listed portions of capacity, not a sales forecast.

Proposed channels are campus clubs, class or dormitory groups and partner shop QR links, where permission is available. These are recruitment options, not confirmed partners. Publish complete pickup conditions before inviting orders. Telegram or Zalo integration can follow a reliable core workflow. Delivery starts only where staff can quote and complete it within the disclosed conditions; no delivery time or free shipping promise is assumed.
